# LabelGuard and the Sustainable Development Goals

LabelGuard supports transparency and accessible label information. These are feature mappings, not claims of measured impact.

| SDG | LabelGuard feature | Intended connection |
|---|---|---|
| 12 — Responsible consumption and production | Label scan, extracted declarations, and readable result checklist | Makes key package information easier for consumers to inspect. |
| 16 — Peace, justice and strong institutions | Rule references, unverified flags, and downloadable scan reports | Makes the basis and uncertainty of a check visible for review. |
| 9 — Industry, innovation and infrastructure | OCR-backed API with replaceable OCR function | Provides a lightweight digital tool that can be extended. |
| 3 — Good health and well-being | Readable label declarations and review prompts | Helps consumers find label information relevant to informed choices. |
| 10 — Reduced inequalities | Mobile-friendly interface, camera input, and English/Hindi/Marathi UI labels | Offers multiple ways to access the label-check workflow. |
| 8 — Decent work and economic growth | Repeatable label checks and scan history | Can support consistent packaging review workflows. |

The app is a screening aid. Its current rules are unverified placeholders and do not establish legal compliance.
